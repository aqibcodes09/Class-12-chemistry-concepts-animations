Electrolysis of Brine for Production of Caustic Soda ($NaOH$), Chlorine ($Cl_2$), and Hydrogen ($H_2$)

Chemical Equations & Reactions:

  • Anode Chamber (Oxidation): $2Cl^- \rightarrow Cl_2 + 2e^-$ (Chlorine gas released)
  • Cathode Chamber (Amalgam Formation): $Na^+ + e^- + Hg \rightarrow Na-Hg$ (Sodium-Mercury Amalgam)
  • Decomposer Chamber: $2Na-Hg + 2H_2O \rightarrow 2NaOH + H_2 + 2Hg$
